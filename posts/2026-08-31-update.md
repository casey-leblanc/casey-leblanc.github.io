In addition, I've been exploring a few project ideas to liven up my website a bit. 

**HUD Glasses**
Pretty much what it sounds like. I wear glasses, and I recently got a smart watch. I think it would be super cool (and educational) to create some sort of attachment that snaps onto my glasses and holographically projects some stuff onto them, maybe a heartbeat monitor that receives data from my watch. 

Later, if it works out, I think I might be able to dabble into computer vision and adding facial recognition onto my glasses. It certainly would help with remembering peoples' names.

Related fields: Electrical engineering, computer science

**Robotic Arm Gantry**
This one might be a bit derivative. A while back I saw this YouTube video describing some guy that made a mechanized grabber that moved around his room on a gantry, and he made it pick up his laundry.

I was thinking I could expand on that. I might be able to make a mechanical arm with three joints that moves around on a gantry secured to my wall, using inverse kinematics to control the arm to do complex movements, although I feel like this one could get really expensive really fast.

Related fields: Robotics, electrical engineering, computer science, mechanical engineering

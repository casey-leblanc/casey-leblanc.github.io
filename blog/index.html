about me

Hi, my name's Casey LeBlanc. I'm a Biological Engineering student at LSU, minoring in Robotics. I love everything to do with technology and how humans interact with it, and I'm constantly trying to acquire as many different tech-related skills as I can. I've got my finger in a whole bunch of pies, so to speak. I'm proficient in multiple different programming languages, CAD, and FFF, SLA, and SLS additive manufacturing techniques, to name a few.
Eventually, I want to break into the field of wearable computing and brain-computer interfaces, but right now I'm just a lowly undergrad.
